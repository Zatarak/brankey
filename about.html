Hi, I'm Brankica. Words have always been my magic wand. Despite my occasional wrestling with spelling, I fall head over heels for the power of words to create entire universes from thin air. Over the years, almost every connection I have made has told me I have a way of bringing forth new views. Their gratitude for my shared impulses and perspectives in return sparked BranKey - my love letter to everything that sets my soul on fire! I might eventually be able to offer something new to this world, but until then, I am here to support you in reflecting on the extraordinary parts of yourself you might not yet see.
