Systemic Coaching

Systems thrive on connection, not correction

What is systemic coaching?


Systemic coaching examines how your work, relationships, and life experiences interconnect. Whether you're seeking personal growth, professional development, or organizational change, these sessions help you see the patterns that shape your decisions and relationships. For companies, this means understanding team dynamics and improving processes naturally. For individuals, it means aligning your career choices with your deeper values.

The process ...


Each session will be a reminder that you are a living system constantly emerging, reconnecting, becoming.

wk 1: Mapping Your Systems

Explore your place within various systems - family, work, society. We'll work with:
• Understanding your different roles
• Identifying patterns of belonging
• Recognizing family and work patterns
• Team dynamics
• Organizational influences

wk 2: Hidden Patterns

Dive into the hidden dynamics that influence your life:
• Mapping emotional patterns
• Understanding unconscious loyalties
• Identifying systemic blocks
• Team and organizational blind spots

wk 3: Relationships Dance

Examine the choreography of your relationships:
• Understanding relationship dynamics
• Team interactions
• Leadership style and expectations
• Boundary setting

wk 4: Resilience

Create your personal narrative of resilience:
• Crafting your personal story
• Building systemic resilience
• Understanding your themes
• Integrating past experiences

wk 5: Radical Compassion

Develop deep compassion for yourself and your systems:
• Self-awareness in contexts
• Understanding systemic empathy
• Forgiveness and culture
• Building sustainable practices

wk 6: Integrated Self

Bring together all aspects of your systemic work:
• Integration of insights
• Creating sustainable change
• Building future vision
• Establishing ongoing practices
