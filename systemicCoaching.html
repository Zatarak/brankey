Systemic Coaching

What is systemic coaching?


These systemic coahing sessions focus on exploring the interactions and relationships that shape you. You, as an observer, create your reality through your perception. This design nurtures self-sustaining being, and the goal is to create a harmony between your internal values and the external world.


The process ...

Each session will be a gentle revolution—not fixing.

Week 1: Whispers of Belonging

Explore your place within various systems - family, work, society. We'll work with:
• Understanding your role in different contexts
• Identifying patterns of belonging
• Recognizing inherited systemic patterns
• Building awareness of your position in various systems

Week 2: Unspoken River Currents

Dive into the hidden dynamics that influence your life:
• Mapping emotional currents in relationships
• Understanding unconscious loyalties
• Identifying systemic blocks
• Exploring generational patterns

Week 3: Dance of Relationships

Examine the choreography of your relationships:
• Understanding relationship dynamics
• Exploring patterns of interaction
• Identifying roles and expectations
• Building healthy boundaries

Week 4: Resilience for Personal Mythology

Create your personal narrative of resilience:
• Crafting your personal story
• Building systemic resilience
• Understanding your life's themes
• Integrating past experiences

Week 5: Radical Compassion

Develop deep compassion for yourself and your systems:
• Practicing self-compassion
• Understanding systemic empathy
• Working with forgiveness
• Building sustainable self-care

Week 6: Integrated Self

Bring together all aspects of your systemic work:
• Integration of insights
• Creating sustainable change
• Building future vision
• Establishing ongoing practices

How to start?

You're not a problem to solve, but a living system constantly emerging, reconnecting, becoming. Take the first step in your transformative journey.

1 Take 10 minutes for guided self-reflection
2 Explore your current systemic patterns
3 Receive personalized insights
