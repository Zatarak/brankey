Community Engagement

Community is a system capable of producing and maintaining itself by creating its own components

What is community engagement?


Community engagement embodies the essence of collective learning and mutual understanding. It is about fostering dialogic interactions where individuals come together to share their experiences and insides, creating space for ontological pluralism. Each individual contributes to the self-generating processes that sustain the whole.

Why community engagement?


Because each human's existence is unique in terms of their experiences, relationships and interactions within various systems. This system is not mearly about participation; it's about nurturing relationships that enable transformative connections among community members, thus fascilitating a deeper understanding of our shared existence and the complexities of our environments.
